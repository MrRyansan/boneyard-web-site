Welcome!

          
You have reached the companion site for The Boneyard, a Mystic BBS kept by sysop Knight Shadow. This is the porch. The real house is still a terminal.

          
Bulletin boards never really died. They went quiet, then people started calling again — not with a modem scream this time, but with telnet and SSH. The Boneyard is one of those places: a late-night hangout for message bases, files, door games, and the kind of conversation that does not refresh itself every twelve seconds.
