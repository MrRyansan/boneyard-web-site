BBS 101 / BBS Culture

Demoscene

The demoscene is a cousin, not a child. It is people writing programs whose only job is to be astonishing: realtime graphics, music, size limits, a party in a gym somewhere in Europe.

Demos, intros, 64k, 4k — the units are filesize and nerve. Cracktros (the little intros on ripped games) are part of the family tree and part of a legal mess; the art form outgrew the crime and became its own circuit of compos, groups, and archives. Pouet, Demozoo, and a thousand stale FTP mirrors later, it is still going.

Where boards come in

BBSes were a distribution layer and a hangout. Scene groups needed places to drop files and argue. Sysops who liked the scene kept file areas for it. ANSI groups and demo groups traded members and aesthetics. If you have ever watched a demo whose credits look like a BBS who-is-online list, that is the overlap in a screenshot.

Watching without a time machine

You can emulate, or you can watch captures. Neither is cheating. The point was always the effect, plus the knowledge that a machine that small did that. If this is your entry point, welcome. Then come back and post about it on the board like a person, not like a recommendation engine.
