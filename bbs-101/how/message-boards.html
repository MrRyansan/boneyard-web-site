BBS 101 / How a BBS Works

Message Boards

This is the original social network, and it still works. You pick a conference, you read, you reply. Threads are not a product. They are a pile of messages with subject lines.

Local vs networked

Local bases live only on this machine. They are the house conversation — the flavor of The Boneyard, the in-jokes, the late-night Japan thread that would make no sense anywhere else.

Networked mail is the old miracle. FidoNet and later FTN-style networks bounce messages from board to board, store-and-forward, like a postal service for nerds. You post here. Someone on another system answers in a day or three. That delay is part of the texture.

Public and private

Public posts are for the conference. Private mail (sometimes called email on the board, sometimes just “mail”) is for one user, including the sysop. Do not put private things in public bases and then act surprised. Do not be weird in sysop mail unless you want a weird reputation.

How to not look like a tourist

  • Read a conference before you post in it.
  • Quote sparingly. Answer the thing that was asked.
  • Handles are costumes, but they are also names. Use them.
  • If the board has an offline mail reader (QWK and friends), that is for heavy readers. You do not need one on day one.

Message bases are where a board gets a soul. Files and doors are the arcade. Mail is the kitchen.
