BBS 101 / How a BBS Works

Chat

Most of a BBS is asynchronous. Chat is the exception: two or more nodes awake at the same time, typing into the same dark.

On a one-line dial-up board, live chat with other users was almost mythical — you had to be the sysop, or the board needed multiple lines. Multi-node telnet boards make it ordinary again. Ordinary, but still small. You will not find ten thousand people. You might find one. That one is the point.

Who you might be talking to

  • Another caller — multi-node chat, teleconference, whatever Mystic named the room.
  • The sysop — paging chat. Use it for real questions, not to test if the doorbell works.
  • Nobody — the usual state. Leave mail instead. The empty chat room is not a failure. It is off-hours.

Etiquette, short

Say hello. Do not dump paste. If someone is in a door, they may not answer a chat ping; they are busy dying in a game. And if Knight Shadow answers a page, you got a human. Be worth the interrupt.

When nobody is online, the message bases are still chat. They are just chat with a longer echo.
