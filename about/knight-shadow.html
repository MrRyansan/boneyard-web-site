Knight Shadow

Every board has a person who keeps the lights on, patches the software, and answers the sysop page at unreasonable hours. Here, that person is Knight Shadow.

A sysop is not a brand manager. They are closer to a lighthouse keeper: mostly invisible, occasionally sharp, responsible for the building even when nobody is in it. Knight Shadow runs The Boneyard on Mystic, sets the tone of the message bases, and decides what kind of night this place is allowed to be.

A note from the other side of the desk

Log in. Read before you write. Do not be a ghost who only downloads. If you are new to boards, say so — people like teaching. If you have been around since the baud-rate wars, you already know: be excellent to the other callers, and do not make me come out of the sysop menu.

Want to talk to the sysop? The board is the right channel. Emailing a website is how you reach a webpage. Paging the sysop is how you reach a person.

Telnet in and leave mail. Handles are cheap. Reputation is not.
