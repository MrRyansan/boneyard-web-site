The Boneyard BBS

The Boneyard is a living bulletin board: not a museum plaque, and not a Discord with extra steps. It is a Mystic system you log into, wander, and leave traces on.

What you will find

Message boards are the heart of the place. Expect conversation that leans into the strange, the nocturnal, and a deep fondness for Japan — film, music, cities at 2 a.m., the whole haunted postcard. Dark topics are welcome when they are thoughtful. Cheap shock is not the same thing as atmosphere.

Around that: file areas, door games, and the usual Mystic furniture. The board is a hangout first. Features exist so people have something to do with their hands while they talk.

Why “The Boneyard”?

A boneyard is where old machines go when the world is finished with them. BBSes were supposed to be finished. They were not. This board keeps the leftover pieces — ANSI, handles, doors, slow mail — and lets them keep working. Nothing here is ironic. Nostalgia is allowed. Living in it is better.

Call in

Telnet or read the connect page if you need a client. New users: pick a handle you can live with. The bones remember.
